Paneer Butter Masala

Paneer Butter Masala is a delicious and creamy Indian dish made with paneer (cottage cheese) in a rich, flavorful tomato-based sauce with butter and spices.

This recipe will guide you through the steps to make this popular dish at home, perfect for serving with naan or rice.

Ingredients:

  • 200g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Steps:

  1. Heat butter in a pan over medium heat. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for another minute.
  3. Add tomato puree, red chili powder, and salt. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  4. Add garam masala and mix well.
  5. Add the paneer cubes and gently mix to coat them with the masala. Cook for 5-7 minutes.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with naan or rice.
